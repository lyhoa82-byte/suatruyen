## CANON_LOCK_001

**Decision ID:** A-3

**Status:** LOCKED

### Locked Canon

Events before Tĩnh Nghi wakes are partly variable.

Some pre-wake events repeat consistently across loops.

Some pre-wake events may differ between loops.

A single observation of a pre-wake event does not prove that the event occurs in every loop.

Future episodes must not assume that all pre-wake events are fixed.

### This decision does not determine

- why variation occurs
- which events are fixed
- which events are variable
- whether memory bleed is real
- whether adaptation exists
- whether another party retains loop knowledge

### Effects on future canon

- A-3 is considered resolved and locked.
- Future canon analysis must treat A-3 as authoritative.
- Future evaluation of A-4 and A-5 must be performed under the assumption that A-3 is locked.

Do not reopen A-3 unless new canon evidence directly contradicts this lock.

## CANON_LOCK_002

**Decision IDs:** A-4A, A-4D, A-4E

**Status:** LOCKED

### A-4A — Gradual Drift

**Locked Canon:**

The palace water clock gains time gradually after adjustment.

The time gap grows over the course of the day.

The observed gap near đầu giờ Mùi is the result of accumulated drift.

**This decision does not determine:**

- the exact adjustment time
- the exact drift rate
- the exact size of the morning gap

### A-4D — Not Necessarily Every Loop

**Locked Canon:**

The clock offset is confirmed in V8 and V9 only.

The presence of a clock adjustment in one loop does not prove the same adjustment occurs in every loop.

The accuracy of palace timestamps in V1–V7 remains undetermined.

**This decision does not determine:**

- which loops contain the adjustment
- what causes the adjustment
- whether future loops contain the adjustment

### A-4E — Outer Watch Is Reference Time

**Locked Canon:**

The outer watch at Tuyên Hòa is the reference time system.

The palace clock can be altered through adjustment.

When the two systems disagree, the outer watch is treated as the authoritative reference.

**This decision does not determine:**

- who orders the adjustment
- who delivers the adjustment slip
- why the adjustment is performed

### Effects on future canon

- A-4 is considered resolved and locked.
- Future evaluation of A-5 must assume CANON_LOCK_001 and CANON_LOCK_002 are authoritative.
- Do not reopen A-4 unless new canon evidence directly contradicts these locks.
